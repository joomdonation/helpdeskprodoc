# Setup

Start with [Set Up Your First Help Desk](/getting-started/first-ticket) if you want to submit and reply to a ticket on a new installation.

- Create [categories](/setup/categories), publish [menu items](/setup/menu-items), and configure [email messages](/setup/email-messages) so customers can open tickets and follow up.
- Add [custom fields](/setup/custom-fields) to collect details specific to your support requests.
- Enable [sensitive data](/setup/sensitive-data) when customers need to share temporary access details that should be deleted when a ticket closes or after a period of inactivity.
- Tailor the workflow with [statuses](/setup/ticket-statuses), [priorities](/setup/ticket-priority), [labels](/setup/ticket-labels), and [pre-defined replies](/setup/pre-defined-replies).
- [Set up a support team](/setup/support-team) when you need to delegate support by category or assign tickets individually.
