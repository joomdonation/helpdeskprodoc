# Setup

Start with [Set Up Your First Help Desk](/getting-started/first-ticket) if you want to submit and reply to a ticket on a new installation.

Work through these pages in order, or jump to the part you need:

1. [Categories](/setup/categories) and [menu items](/setup/menu-items): give customers a form and a place to follow up.
2. [Email messages](/setup/email-messages): control the notifications Helpdesk Pro sends.
3. [Support team](/setup/support-team): delegate by category or assign tickets individually.
4. [Custom fields](/setup/custom-fields): collect details specific to each kind of request.
5. [Statuses](/setup/ticket-statuses), [priorities](/setup/ticket-priority), [labels](/setup/ticket-labels), and [pre-defined replies](/setup/pre-defined-replies): tailor the workflow.
6. [Sensitive data](/setup/sensitive-data): let customers share temporary access details that are deleted when a ticket closes or after a period of inactivity.
