When you first install the extension, Helpdesk Pro will populate default Emails & Messages (which will be sent to admin/manager/staff/customer when a support ticket is submitted/replied). You can access to Helpdesk Pro -> Emails & Messages to adjust these messages to meet your own need if needed.

1. New ticket admin email subject
2. New ticket admin email body
3. New ticket user email subject
4. New ticket user email body
5. Ticket updated admin email subject	
6. Ticket updated admin email body
7. Ticket updated user email subject
8. Ticket updated user email body
9. Ticket assigned email subject
10. Ticket assigned email body
