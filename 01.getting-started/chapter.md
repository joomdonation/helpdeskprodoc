# Getting Started

New to Helpdesk Pro? Follow the pages in this chapter in order:

1. [Introduction](/getting-started/introduction): what Helpdesk Pro does and who it is for.
2. [Basic Concept](/getting-started/the-concept): what happens from the first message to closure.
3. [User Roles](/getting-started/user-roles): who does what (customer, administrator, manager, staff).
4. [Installation](/getting-started/installation): install, update, and move the extension.
5. [Set Up Your First Help Desk](/getting-started/first-ticket): publish a category, add a menu link, and test a conversation from both sides.
6. [Configuration](/getting-started/configuration): review every global setting.
