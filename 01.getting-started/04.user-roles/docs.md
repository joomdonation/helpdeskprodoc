In a basic help desk, customers submit tickets and administrators reply. You can also appoint managers to oversee specific categories or assign individual tickets to staff members.

| Role | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | Submit support tickets and follow up on their own tickets. |
| **Administrator** | Manage and reply to tickets in all categories. |
| **Manager** (optional) | Manage and reply to tickets in one or more assigned categories. |
| **Staff** (optional) | View and reply to tickets that an administrator or category manager assigns to them. |
