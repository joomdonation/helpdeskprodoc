Helpdesk Pro is a support ticket and knowledge base extension for Joomla. It gives customers a place to ask for help on your website and gives you the tools to organize requests, reply, and follow each issue through to resolution.

Each ticket keeps the original request, follow-up messages, and attachments together in one conversation. Customers can return to their tickets to check progress and provide more information. Your support team can see the conversation before replying, rather than searching through separate email exchanges.

## Who Is It For?

Use Helpdesk Pro when you want to manage customer or member support within your Joomla site. For example, a business can organize requests by product or service, a membership site can answer member questions, and a technical support team can collect details and screenshots needed to investigate problems.

You can start with a simple help desk where one administrator answers every request. As your workload grows, you can give managers responsibility for particular categories and assign individual tickets to staff members. You can also publish a knowledge base so visitors can find answers to common questions themselves.

## Start Simple, Add a Team When Needed

Helpdesk Pro supports four roles: **customers** who submit tickets, **administrators** who configure the help desk and manage every ticket, and two optional roles, **managers** (responsible for one or more categories) and **staff** (who work on tickets assigned to them). See [User Roles](/getting-started/user-roles) for what each role can do.

Managers and staff are not required for a working help desk. The [first help desk guide](/getting-started/first-ticket) uses only an administrator and a customer. Add the optional roles when you need to share support responsibilities.

## What Can You Do with Helpdesk Pro?

- **Collect the information you need.** Organize requests into [categories](/setup/categories), allow attachments, and use [custom fields](/setup/custom-fields) to ask questions relevant to each category. You can require customers to sign in or allow guest submission, depending on your configuration.
- **Organize and resolve tickets.** Find requests using filters, set [statuses](/setup/ticket-statuses) and [priorities](/setup/ticket-priority), add [labels](/setup/ticket-labels), and assign work to your team. Administrators can manage tickets in Joomla's administrator area; customers, managers, and staff use the website according to their roles.
- **Keep conversations moving.** Email notifications tell customers and support users about relevant ticket activity. [Pre-defined replies](/setup/pre-defined-replies) help your team reuse common answers. With the [email ticketing plugin](/create-and-reply-tickets-via-email) configured, incoming emails can create tickets or add replies to existing conversations.
- **Handle temporary access details.** Enable [Sensitive Data](/setup/sensitive-data) to collect credentials separately from ordinary messages. These fields are encrypted in the database and cleared when the ticket closes. A scheduled task can also remove them from inactive tickets while keeping the conversation.
- **Offer self-service answers.** Publish a searchable [knowledge base](/knowledge-base) with articles grouped by topic. You can write articles directly or reuse suitable ticket conversations after reviewing their content for publication.
- **Understand support demand.** Use the [Dashboard](/manage-tickets/dashboard) to follow ticket volume over time and compare categories. [Reports and email logs](/manage-tickets/reports-and-email-logs) help you review ticket totals, support replies, and notification messages.
- **Automate routine maintenance.** Use [scheduled tasks](/scheduled-tasks) to close inactive tickets or remove old attachments, sensitive data, and whole tickets according to your retention settings.
- **Support a multilingual site.** Translate categories, statuses, email messages, and other supported content for the languages used on your website. See [Multilingual Setup](/multilingual).

## Where to Start

Read [Basic Concept](/getting-started/the-concept) and [User Roles](/getting-started/user-roles), then [install Helpdesk Pro](/getting-started/installation) and follow [Set Up Your First Help Desk](/getting-started/first-ticket) to submit a test request, reply as the administrator, and close the ticket.

Once that works, choose the features your site needs. Add a [support team](/setup/support-team), configure [email ticketing](/create-and-reply-tickets-via-email), or publish a [knowledge base](/knowledge-base). Optional features require their own configuration; installing Helpdesk Pro does not automatically enable every workflow described above.
